Matteo Nerini

👋🏻 Welcome to my webpage!

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Imperial College London, UK, and my research interests include signal processing and wireless communications.

I received my BSc (2018) and MSc (2020) from the University of Bologna, Italy, an additional MSc (2020) from NTNU - Norwegian University of Science and Technology, Norway, and my PhD (2024) from Imperial College London, UK. In 2020, I also received the Licence of the Collegio Superiore, the school of excellence of the University of Bologna, which selects approximately the top 0.1% of students. In 2023, during my PhD, I was a Visiting Researcher at the Technical University of Munich, Germany.

I was awarded the Eryl Cadwallader Davies Prize in 2025 for the best PhD thesis in the Electrical and Electronic Engineering Department at Imperial, the Imperial College London PhD Prize by G-Research in 2025 for the second-best PhD thesis at Imperial, and the IEEE GLOBECOM 2025 Best Paper Award.

If you have any questions or want to exchange some ideas, I am always happy to chat. Please reach me at m.nerini20 [at] imperial.ac.uk.
